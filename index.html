About the studio

Independent studio.
One game — one immersion.

DeebSilk Studio is an indie studio devoted to immersive RPG experiences. We build Deep Dive Online by hand: from idea to class balance, from Blender models to the combat system in Unity 6. No corporate layer — just development, lore and community.

While development is under way, you can already dive into the world: study the wiki, explore the map, try the combat mechanics in the minigame or support the project.

The game

Deep Dive Online

Deep Dive Online drops players into a next-generation virtual reality. Legend says the world was woven from stardust and celestial light. But a mysterious rift opened portals to the lower worlds, and monsters poured through. Now the fate of this world lies with the Players — can you reach floor 100 and uncover the secret of the creator?

What already lives in the game

01

Dynamic combat

Real-time combo attacks, dodges and magic abilities. Every class feels different.

02

Inventory system

Hundreds of items, stacks, weight and quick access. Bring back everything you find in dungeons.

Equipment

Sets, bonuses, crafting and item upgrades. Look and stats — your way to play.

NPC dialogue

Story campaign and endless repeatable missions. Rewards grow with every floor.

Monsters learn: they adapt to your style, call for backup and set traps on your path.

FAQ

Questions & answers

When will the game be released?
We are heading to Alpha now. The release date depends on how fast we finish the core systems and the first full world — follow the roadmap and blog.
Will there be VR support?
Yes. OpenXR is our VR standard, with Meta Quest as the primary headset target. VR mode is planned for the Beta stage.
Can I play already?
You can try the combat system right now in the minigame. Closed testing will open with the Alpha stage.
Will the game be free to play?
The final business model is still being decided. The donation page supports development today, and every supporter gets an in-game title.
How can I support the project?
Join the Discord, share the game with friends, or buy a pack on the donation page — all funds go to servers and new artists.
